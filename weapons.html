Weapon progression

Your ranged weapon is your main damage. Upgrade it first, every time. Melee is mostly a stat stick: a 2-hander with Agility/Stamina, plus Raptor Strike damage when something reaches you.

Forever adds 1,000+ new quests and new items, and has rebalanced some old ones (e.g. Venomstrike is buffed). The list below has only items whose name and source were checked against Classic/Forever data. Expect new Forever quest rewards that beat some of these, so always compare the DPS on the tooltip.

Ammo & quivers

Vanilla-style ammo: bows and crossbows fire arrows, guns fire bullets. Ammo damage adds directly to every shot, so always buy the best ammo you can use. A quiver (arrows) or ammo pouch (bullets) goes in a bag slot and gives ranged haste. Upgrade it when you can afford to.
