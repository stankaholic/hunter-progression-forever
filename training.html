Spells by trainer level

Must-have spells are marked. Rank levels are vanilla 1.12. Forever marks a first-learn level confirmed different on WoW Forever. Forever reworked talents and some spells, so if a rank is missing or early, trust the trainer window.

Budget tip: you can skip rank-ups you rarely use (Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite, Distracting Shot, Scare Beast, extra Tracking) when money is tight. Never skip Serpent Sting, Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot, Aimed Shot, Aspect of the Hawk or Mend Pet.

Horde hunter trainers

Any hunter trainer teaches every rank. Starting-area trainers are just the closest ones.

The Crossroads and Undercity have no confirmed vanilla hunter trainer. Plan trainer trips around Orgrimmar or Thunder Bluff (Zeppelins from Durotar reach Tirisfal and Grom'gol). Undercity is still worth visiting for Polearms.

Weapon masters & skills

Weapon skills cost 10s each (Polearms: level 20, 1g). Hunters can't use maces, so don't learn them.

Weapon masterLocationTeachesLearn?
HanashiValley of Honor, OrgrimmarBows, Thrown, Axes, StavesBows, Axes, Staves
SayocValley of Honor, OrgrimmarBows, Thrown, Axes, Staves, Daggers, Fist WeaponsSame as Hanashi, plus Daggers / Fist if you dual wield (Forever: Dual Wield at 20)
AnsekhwaLower Rise, Thunder BluffGuns, Maces, StavesGuns, Staves
ArchibaldWar Quarter, UndercityCrossbows, Daggers, Swords, PolearmsPolearms (lvl 20), Crossbows, Swords

Recommendation: learn all three ranged types (Bows, Guns, Crossbows) so you can take any ranged upgrade. For melee, hunters want a slow 2-hander for Agility/Stamina and Raptor Strike: Staves, Polearms, 2H Swords or 2H Axes. Racial bonuses: Trolls get +5 Bows, Orcs +5 Axes.

Pet trainer (Beast Training)

Pet trainers in Orgrimmar and Thunder Bluff sell the passive and utility skills below. You teach them yourself with the Beast Training window, which costs pet training points (TP). Pets earn more TP as they level and gain loyalty. Bite, Claw, Dash, Dive, Cower, Prowl, etc. are not sold. Your pet learns them by taming a beast that knows them (see Pets).
