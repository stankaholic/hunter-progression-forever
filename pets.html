Pet skills: Claw & Dash

Claw is your main pet damage skill. Dash lets your pet reach targets fast. Neither is sold by trainers. You learn each rank by taming a beast that already knows it. Enter your level in the header to highlight beasts in your level range.

How to learn a skill from a beast

  1. Find a teacher. Beast Lore (level 24) shows which abilities a beast knows. You can only tame beasts at or below your own level.
  2. Stable your main pet at a stable master, or abandon a throwaway pet, then Tame Beast on the teacher.
  3. Fight with it. Keep the skill on autocast and kill a few mobs. Eventually you'll see "You have learned a new spell: Claw (Rank N)". Dash can take many fights, because pets don't always use it.
  4. Swap back. Abandon the teacher (right-click the pet portrait), then take your main pet out of the stable.
  5. Teach it. Open Beast Training and spend TP. Your pet must be at least the rank's pet level and have enough training points.
Who can use what: Claw: cats, bears, crabs, owls, raptors, scorpids, carrion birds. Dash: cats, wolves, hyenas, boars. Cats get both, so a cat is the classic leveling DPS pet.

Recommended Horde pet path

LevelStep
10Do the pet quest in Durotar or Mulgore. Then tame an Encrusted Surf Crawler or Venomtail Scorpid (Durotar) to learn Claw 2. Tauren can use the Ferocious Grizzled Bear in Silverpine (11–12), but Durotar is closer.
13–19Keep the pet level with you (feed it, keep it happy). Any Barrens cat (Savannah lions) is a fine main pet for now.
20–26Claw 3 from Ashenvale (Clattering Crawler / Ashenvale Bear) or Hillsbrad (Gray Bear). Claw 4 from the Elder Ashenvale Bear (25–26).
30–35Tame a Stranglethorn Tiger or Kurzen War Tiger (32–33) for Dash 1. It can be your new main cat. Claw 5 from Vile Sting (Thousand Needles) or Scorpashi Lasher (Desolace).
40–45King Bangalash (43, Stranglethorn) knows Claw 6 + Dash 2. Taming him is a strong upgrade on its own. Otherwise: Elder Shadowmaw Panther (Dash 2) plus a Feralas or Swamp of Sorrows Claw 6 teacher.
48–52Claw 7 from Ironfur Patriarch (Feralas) or the Felwood bears/owls. Dash 3 from the Vilebranch Raiding Wolf (Hinterlands, 50–51).
56–60Claw 8 from Elder Shardtooth or Winterspring Screecher. Endgame cat: Frostsaber Stalker or Frostsaber Huntress (Winterspring, Dash 3).
